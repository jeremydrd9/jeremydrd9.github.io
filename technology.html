How AcneTrack reads your skin.

A face scan, a detailed profile and 20+ factors, combined into one plan. Here is what happens behind the scenes.

From scan to plan

Four steps, each one building on the last.

  1. 1

    Face scan

    Your photo is analyzed by AI to read your skin: the type of acne, the zones affected and how inflamed it looks.

  2. 2

    Your profile

    Your answers add what a photo cannot show: skin type, routine, diet, sleep, stress, cycle and current treatments.

  3. 3

    Cause analysis

    Scan and profile are weighed against 20+ factors, from hormones to skincare habits, to name the most likely main cause of your acne.

  4. 4

    Your plan

    Everything becomes your plan: acne score, goal date, priorities and a routine with products picked for your skin and your budget.
