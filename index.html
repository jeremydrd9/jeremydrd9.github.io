FAQ

Questions about tracking your acne

How the method works — and what to realistically expect from it.

Why does the AcneTrack method work?

Because it treats acne as a problem with a cause, rather than a surface to cover up. Most routines attack the spot that is already there while whatever produced it carries on untouched.

AcneTrack works the other way round. It builds a profile of your skin, gives you a routine to follow daily, and measures the result with a score you can compare over time. That loop — act, measure, adjust — is what turns guesswork into something you can steer. When your score moves, you know what moved it.

Do I need to know what's causing my acne before I start?

No — finding that out is the whole point. Most people have a theory about their acne and no way to test it.

AcneTrack tracks more than twenty possible causes, from hormones and stress to sleep, skincare and diet. As you use it, the app narrows them down and surfaces the one most likely to be driving your breakouts, so you stop treating everything at once and start treating the thing that actually matters.

I've already tried everything. Why would this be different?

If products alone were the answer, they would have worked by now. What usually fails is not the product — it is that nothing was ever measured, so there was no way to tell what helped, what did nothing, and what quietly made things worse.

AcneTrack is not another cream. It gives you a way to see your skin objectively: a score that moves, a history you can scroll back through, and a cause you can finally name. It will not clear your skin overnight, but it stops you repeating the same cycle blind.

How does the AI acne scan work?

You take a photo of your skin and AcneTrack analyses it in seconds, returning your acne score out of 100, the type of acne it detects, and tailored advice. For close-up analysis of a single spot, the Pimple Scan uses CE-marked medical software built to ISO 13485 quality standards.

Each new scan is compared with your previous ones, so the Evolution tab shows whether your skin is actually improving rather than relying on memory.

Can the food I eat really cause acne?

For some people, yes. Research has linked high-glycaemic foods and dairy to breakouts, but triggers are highly individual — what wrecks one person's skin does nothing to another's.

Rather than hand you a generic list of things to avoid, AcneTrack lets you scan a meal and tells you how well it fits your skin profile — before you eat it. Diet is one of the causes the app weighs when narrowing down what is driving your breakouts.

How long does it take to see results?

Skin renews itself over roughly four to six weeks, so meaningful change rarely shows up sooner than that — and any product or app promising clear skin in days is overselling.

Most users who track consistently start seeing measurable movement in their acne score after about eight to twelve weeks. Consistency matters far more than intensity, which is why the app is built around a daily routine and a streak.

Does AcneTrack work for hormonal acne?

Hormonal patterns are one of the causes AcneTrack tracks, alongside diet, sleep, stress, skincare and around twenty others. Logging consistently helps surface cyclical patterns that are hard to spot day to day.

That said, hormonal acne often needs medical treatment. AcneTrack helps you understand and document what is happening to your skin — it is not a replacement for a dermatologist.
